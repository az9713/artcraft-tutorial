How the first Lumen spot score was made, and why it failed

Lumen Coffee Roasters — Ember Ridge :30 launch spot · production test of the Artcraft craft apps · 2026-10-09

effectcraft did not design the sound. The build script projects/effectcraft/build_motion.jsx:192-208 set every note, length and level. The agent wrote that script in the previous session. effectcraft only rendered what the script told it. So the bad sound is a script-design error, not an effectcraft defect.

What the script told effectcraft to do

  1. Make a 30 s composition named "Music Bed 30s".
  2. Add 4 solid layers. Put effectcraft's Tone effect on each one. Tone is a test-tone generator with 5 oscillators. Each oscillator holds one frequency without change.
  3. Set the waveform to sine. The script sets property(1) to 1. In After Effects' Tone effect, which effectcraft copies, value 1 is sine. This was not confirmed in effectcraft's documentation.
  4. Give each layer one 5-note chord for 7.5 s (table below).
  5. Keyframe the level from 0 up to 9 and back to 0, with 0.75 s crossfades between chords. The first chord fades in over 2.0 s. The last chord fades out from 27 s to 30 s.

0–7.5 sDmaj9146.83, 220.00, 277.18, 329.63, 369.99
7.5–15 sBm11123.47, 185.00, 220.00, 293.66, 329.63

effectcraft rendered the result to projects/effectcraft/out/renders/lumen-temp-score-30s.wav. The file is 5,760,044 bytes, which is 30 s of 48 kHz 16-bit stereo plus a 44-byte header. filmcraft (projects/filmcraft/build_spot.py) put it on track A1 at −6 dB. The :30 export normalised it to −16 LUFS, and the 9:16 :15 export normalised it to −14 LUFS, with a 45-frame audio fade.

Why it sounds like a monotone noise

  • Sine waves are not instruments. A sine wave has no overtones. Each note sounds like a hearing-test beep.
  • Nothing moves. There is no melody, no rhythm and no note attack. The sound changes 4 times in 30 s, once every 7.5 s. Between changes, it is one steady hum.
  • Every chord buzzes. Each chord has two notes only a whole tone apart. Two sine waves that close together make a beat at their difference frequency. Between about 20 and 75 Hz, the ear hears that beat as a rough buzz:
ChordClose pair (Hz)Beat frequency
Dmaj9369.99 − 329.6340.36 Hz
Bm11329.63 − 293.6635.97 Hz

So a buzz continues through all 30 s. This is the most probable cause of the "noise". It was not measured in the old WAV; it follows from the frequencies in the script.

Why it does not fit the materials

Chord change on a cut (15 s) Chord change inside a shot (7.5 s, 22.5 s)
  • It ignores the cuts. The picture cuts at 5, 9, 15, 19, 23 and 26 s. The chords change at 7.5, 15 and 22.5 s. Only the change at 15 s falls on a cut.
  • There is no sound for the logo. The logo sting animates from 0 to 1.9 s: the sun, the bean, then the wordmark. During that time, the first chord is still fading in over 2.0 s, so the logo appears almost in silence.
  • There is no ending. The end card (26–30 s) gets a slow fade from 27 s, not a final chord or accent.
  • The style is wrong for the brand. The materials show a warm, hand-made product: an Ethiopian single origin and a named head roaster. A steady electronic drone gives no warmth and no human sound.

Why the script used Tone

The previous session needed music built only from craft apps. Tone was the sound generator it used. It is not known if effectcraft has a better audio source, such as a sampler or MIDI instruments. The command catalogues in tools/cat/ hold no effectcraft effect list, so this was not checked.

What replaced it

The spot now uses a 30 s instrumental jingle from ElevenLabs Music v2.5: projects/filmcraft/music/lumen-jingle-30s.mp3, take C of 3 in projects/filmcraft/music/takes/. It has marimba, acoustic guitar, upright bass and light percussion at about 100 BPM. The music is therefore no longer craft-made. The old "Music Bed 30s" composition and lumen-temp-score-30s.wav are still in the effectcraft project, but the spot does not use them.
